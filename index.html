About King of Floors

A Craftsman's Standard, Built Over Two Continents

King of Floors was founded by Kleber Farias, who spent four years installing floors in the Netherlands before bringing that same European precision to Atlanta. Today, with 7+ years of hands-on experience and crews working across Georgia and North Carolina, King of Floors brings a level of care most companies outgrow: every plank, stair, and panel installed like it's going in his own home.
